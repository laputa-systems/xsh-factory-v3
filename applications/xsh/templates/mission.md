# XSH mission

Make XSH the practical, typed shell language described by its own contracts: predictable enough for
everyday automation, clear enough to review, and honest at Unix and process boundaries.

This factory improves XSH only through reproducible, user-visible behavior defects. Every proposal
starts from the current clean product checkout, carries one exact two-run failure reproducer, names
the public contract it affects, and remains independently reviewable through local delivery. A
refactor, cleanup preference, speculative feature, dependency update, performance project, or
documentation-only task is not product work for this campaign.

Correctness is the goal; throughput is a constraint. The ticket buffer may create pressure to
search, but never pressure to lower evidence, duplicate work, broaden scope, or treat prose as a
passing test.
